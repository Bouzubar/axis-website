Who this is for

Athletes at least 6 months after ACL reconstruction who have been discharged by their physiotherapist, or cleared to run and jump, and want a structured, tested path back to their sport.

Physiotherapy gets the knee working again. This program covers what usually comes after it, when people are told "you can start training" and left to guess: getting the strength back to match the other leg, landing and cutting with control, and sprinting at full speed again.

Not for: revision surgery, more than one ligament reconstructed, a meniscus or cartilage repair with restrictions still in place, under 16 without a parent's and clinician's sign-off, or a knee that still swells after training.

Your week

Four days a week, in this order. Field days come first so your legs are fresh for sprints and landings.

Sun Mon Tue Wed Thu Fri Sat
Day 1 Field Day 2 Gym Rest Day 3 Field Day 4 Gym Rest Rest

How it runs:

  1. Entry Check first. If any line is not true, you don't start.
  2. Test before Week 1. Strength, hop and single-leg squat tests, plus a confidence questionnaire.
  3. Check the knee every morning. Same size as the other knee, and it straightens fully. A pain and swelling table tells you what to do if it doesn't.
  4. Pass each gate to move on. If you don't pass, you repeat the last 2 weeks and retest.

The rules that make it work

  • Tests decide, not the calendar. You only move to faster, harder work once the leg has shown it is ready.
  • The other leg counts too. If your other leg gets weaker, the injured side looks better than it is. The PDF shows how to correct for that.
  • Swelling is the signal. Distance and speed go up only after a session with no swelling the next morning.

How you'll measure progress

At Week 0, Week 4, Week 8 and Week 12 you test:

  • Strength: single-leg leg extension and leg curl, each leg.
  • Hops: single, triple, crossover and 6 m timed hops, each leg.
  • Control: a filmed single-leg squat to a box.
  • Confidence: the ACL-RSI questionnaire, a published 12-question scale of how ready you feel.

Each leg is compared with the other as a percentage. You record everything on the score sheet in the PDF.

Exit standard at Week 12: at least 9 months since surgery, strength and all four hops at 90% or more of the other leg, the knee staying in line on the single-leg squat, an ACL-RSI of 65 or more, and two weeks of non-contact team training with no swelling. Then you take the score sheet to your surgeon or physiotherapist. The decision to return to matches is made with them.

Before you buy: you must pass this entry check

All of these must be true before you start:

  • At least 6 months since surgery.
  • Discharged by your physiotherapist, or written clearance to run and jump.
  • You can run 20 minutes at an easy pace with no pain and no swelling the next morning.
  • Your knee straightens fully, the same as the other side, and bends to within a hand's width of the other side.
  • No swelling, and no giving way since surgery.

Stop and contact your surgeon or physiotherapist if the knee gives way, locks or catches, swells for more than 24 hours, or you get sharp pain on a landing or cut, or calf pain and swelling in the lower leg.

This program does not replace your surgeon or physiotherapist. When you buy, you confirm that you are at least 6 months after ACL reconstruction and have been cleared to run and jump.
